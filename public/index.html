Waiting in a queue?

Answer a few quick questions about this place while you wait and pick up a reward at the counter.

How it works

  1. Scan the CrowdFeed code or tap the NFC tag where you are queueing.
  2. Answer at least five short questions. The questions adapt to the place, the time and your earlier answers.
  3. Show the QR code you receive at the counter to get the reward. You can save it and use it later.
